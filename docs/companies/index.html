Could a short weekly check-in help your staff?

CalmCampus is a weekly wellbeing check-in for students, where answers stay on the phone. We would like to talk with employers about whether a version for the workplace could help. We are at the very start of this.

Book a 20-minute call
Where we are

An honest starting point.

There is no workplace product yet, and no company is using CalmCampus. What exists today is the student app. We would like to hear what staff wellbeing looks like in your organisation before we decide whether and how to build anything.

What we would like to talk about

  • What you already offer your staff, and where there are gaps.
  • Whether a short, private weekly check-in would be welcome or not.
  • What would make employees trust it, and what would make them not.

The same privacy idea

  • Answers would stay on the employee's phone.
  • No accounts and no analytics, as in the student app.
  • Nothing about an individual would reach the employer.

These are intentions to discuss, not a finished offer. We will not promise features that do not exist.

What we do not claim

  • CalmCampus is a wellness tool, not a medical device. It does not diagnose or treat anything.
  • We have no results for workplaces, and none for the student app yet.
  • It does not replace occupational health, a doctor or professional care.
